# UMK-1962: void a full sale (CASHAC), server part

Unit: U3 follow-up (epic plan Rulings: "void/refund not done in U3/U5"). Linear: UMK-1962. UI is a later change.
Payment spec: payment-api (UMK-1991) exists, but pos-api does not call it yet; this change updates `pos` state only.

## Goal

A paid CASHAC sale (cash, EDC debit, the store's static QRIS/transfer, kasbon) can be voided in full, once. The
original sale and its lines stay as they are; the sale gets status `void` and a reversing record. In one
transaction stock goes back into the batches it came from, the voucher use goes back, a kasbon's receivable is
cancelled, the cash that the customer gets back leaves the drawer of the shift that is open now, and an audit row
says who started it, who approved it, why and when. Reports and the sales summary leave the voided sale out.

## Rulings (owner, 24 Nov 2026, from UMK-1962 and the hand-off)

- Full void only. No line, quantity or amount in the request (strict schema).
- **Approval:** the approver holds `sales.void` and types a 6-digit void PIN. The approver is a staff member or
  the owner; an approver may approve their own void. The person who starts the void needs `sales.create` (the
  cashier starts, an admin approves on the same device).
- **Void PIN:** one per actor (staff or owner), scrypt-hashed (`staff/secrets.ts`), never returned or logged.
  An actor with `sales.void` sets and changes their own PIN (the current PIN is required for a change). Five
  wrong PINs lock that approver for 5 minutes (counter on the PIN row, under a row lock); attempts during the
  lock do not extend it; after the lock the count starts again at 0. The owner can unlock an approver and reset
  a forgotten PIN. An approver without a PIN gives a clear 422.
- **Reason:** one of `wrong_item_or_qty`, `wrong_payment_method`, `customer_cancelled`,
  `duplicate_transaction`, `goods_returned`, `other` (the same codes as payment-api). `other` needs a note.
- **Money back:**
  - cash sale: the sale total leaves the drawer of the actor's OPEN shift as a ledger entry (`void_refund`,
    out) through `recordShiftCash`. No open shift: 422 "Buka shift dulu untuk membatalkan struk tunai". A
    closed shift is never changed, also when the sale belongs to it.
  - debit / wallet (transfer, QRIS, EDC): recorded as returned by transfer. No ledger entry, no shift needed.
  - kasbon: the remaining receivable is cancelled; every payment received (down payment and instalments) is
    recorded as returned, cash ones out of the open shift's drawer, the others by transfer. An unpaid kasbon
    returns nothing and needs no shift.
- **Idempotency:** `Idempotency-Key` is required. The same key with the same request returns the first result
  (without asking the PIN again); the same key with another request is 422; another key on a voided sale is 409.
- **Reports:** dashboard, sales, product and shift reports and the sales list summary exclude voided sales. A
  shift's cash figures keep the cash sale (the money did come in) and show the return as cash out.
- Order of checks: validation, replay, sale state, open shift, then the PIN. A request that cannot succeed
  never costs a PIN attempt.

## Definition of Done

- [test] `POST /sales/:id/void` voids a cash sale: status `void`, stock back in its batches at their cost,
  voucher quota back, ledger `void_refund` out, expected cash down by the total, sale and line rows unchanged.
- [test] Stock from two FIFO batches returns to the same batches (cost preserved).
- [test] A sale from a closed shift: the closed shift's row is unchanged, cash leaves the open shift.
- [test] Cash sale with no open shift: 422, nothing changes, no PIN attempt counted.
- [test] Debit sale: voided without a ledger entry and without an open shift.
- [test] Kasbon unpaid, partly paid (cash and debit instalments) and fully paid; a payment on a cancelled
  receivable is refused.
- [test] Replay with the same key returns the first result once; key reused for another sale 422; second void
  409; missing key 422; two parallel voids: exactly one wins.
- [test] Approver matrix: no `sales.void`, unknown, other store, deactivated: refused like a wrong PIN, no
  counter; no PIN: clear 422; self-approval and owner approval work.
- [test] PIN: format 422, set needs `sales.void` (403), change needs the current PIN, hashes are salted and
  never in a response; 5 wrong tries lock for 300 s; a correct PIN during the lock is refused; parallel guesses
  cannot beat the counter; only the owner unlocks or resets.
- [test] Validation: unknown reason, `other` without a note, partial-void fields, bad PIN shape: 422 with no
  PIN attempt.
- [test] Reports (dashboard, sales, products, shifts) and the sales list summary exclude the voided sale.
- [test] Cross-tenant: store B gets 404 on store A's sale (void, read) and on store A's approver (unlock,
  reset).
- [test] Guards: the void PIN table is touched only in `src/void/void-pin.ts`; the ledger guard still holds
  (both proven revert-red).
- [cmd] `npm run lint && npm run typecheck && npm run build`, `PGTEST_DB=pos_u1962 npx jest --runInBand`.
- [qa] Not in this change: the "Batalkan struk" screens (UMK-1962 UI), payment-api call.
