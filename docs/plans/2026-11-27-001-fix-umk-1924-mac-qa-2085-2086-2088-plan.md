# Mac QA fixes: UMK-2085, UMK-2086, UMK-2088 (PR 70)

Epic UMK-1924. Small fixes from the local M2 browser QA of 26 Nov 2026. No migration.

## Goal

Close the display bugs the QA run found and two server figures that were wrong or ambiguous: an open shift's expected cash, and the
product report's Total row.

## Definition of Done

- [test] `GET /shifts/:id`, `GET /shifts` and `GET /reports/shifts` answer an open shift's expected cash (opening cash + cash sales + cash in - cash out),
  equal to `GET /shifts/current`, and the stored value after the close (`test/shift-cash.e2e-spec.ts`; reverting the report change turns it red).
  One computation: `ShiftsService.expectedCashOf`, also used by the report.
- [test] `GET /reports/products`: `totals.grossSales` and `totals.discount` keep their meaning (same as `/reports/sales`); new `netSales` (sum of the rows,
  net of line discounts) and `orderDiscount`; `netSales - orderDiscount - voucher + tax = revenue`. The CSV's Total row is `netSales` and its discount line is
  "Diskon pesanan" (`test/reports.e2e-spec.ts`).
- [test] Riwayat shift shows the cashier's name (`shiftCashier`); one `rupiah()` in `lib/rupiah.ts` writes "−Rp32.000"; `isNotFoundError` / `parentPath`
  (`lib/not-found.ts`) pick the not-found page.
- [test] Laporan kasir has the columns Pembayaran piutang, Kas masuk, Kas keluar (the checklist was right, the page was incomplete).
- [qa] At 375 and 1024: the current report tab is visible in the tab bar; the six OTP boxes sit inside the card's padding; a not-found detail page offers a
  full-width "Kembali" to the parent list, not "Coba lagi".
- [manual] UMK-2086 items 2 and 3 (accessible names): the vendored OtpInput already labels its single input "Kode verifikasi" and the phone field is labelled
  "Nomor WhatsApp"; both were read from the DOM. Probably artefacts of the QA tool; no code change.
