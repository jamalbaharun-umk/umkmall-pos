## Open

- **O1 — fixed in a263f08 by ui-parity-a** (catalog product form kept raw text and validates with
  `check.wholeNumber`; re-checked in the browser). Remark for the owner: the product form refuses "5.000" while the
  cashier and shift forms accept thousand dots ("100.000"); one rule for rupiah fields would be less surprising. Done: f287de4 (product form accepts grouped dots) and the
  cashier's `parseRupiah` now accepts only plain digits or grouped thousands (D12, same commit as this line).
- **O2 — Lakasir fields not in the POS model** (unchanged from the plan): product stock/image on cashier cards,
  purchase number/date/due date/image/payment status, opname number/PIC, store name on the receipt, A4 invoice.
  Recommendation: a follow-up unit once the owner wants them; nothing breaks without them.
- **O3 — not-found pages are served with HTTP 500** (shared error boundary throws a plain `Error`). The page reads
  correctly; recommendation: throw `notFound()` for 404 answers in the loaders (shared, all screens).
- **O4 — the shared confirm dialog's button is always red**, also for approvals (Lakasir uses the primary colour).
  Recommendation: a `tone` option on `components/confirm.tsx` (ui-parity-a).
- Supplier names may repeat (no unique rule, as in Lakasir): left as is.
