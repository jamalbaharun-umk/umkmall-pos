## Differences from Lakasir (by design, not defects)

- Lakasir's report header carries the store name; the POS has no store name setting yet (shell shows "Toko kamu").
- Lakasir prints a Total row inside the table; the POS lists totals under it (same figures, plus tax and revenue).
- Lakasir's per-item "net profit" uses per-item discounts, which the POS does not have; sale-level discount and tax
  appear in the totals.
- Lakasir offers "Download as PDF"; the POS prints through the browser (no server PDF) and offers CSV.
- The cashier report is per shift with cash reconciliation (Lakasir: per sale); per-sale cashier view is the sales
  report's Kasir column.
